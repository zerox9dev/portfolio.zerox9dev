## Task

I ran into the problem myself: finding a remote job means digging through dozens of boards, each with its own filters and its own noise. Half of the “remote” roles turn out to be “US only” in the fine print, and the same opening is reposted on five sites. I wanted one place that reads jobs at the source and tells you honestly whether you can actually take them.

## Solution

I did the whole product cycle on my own — from design to development. HireClover started as Znaidy, a board for designers, and grew into a full aggregator. The second version was rebuilt from scratch: jobs are now collected straight from company hiring systems — the public boards of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and others — then normalised, deduplicated and checked for where each company actually hires.

The way in is Clover, an AI job search agent. You describe what you want in one line — “Python senior from Spain, $5k+” — and Clover shows the open roles that fit. You can try it without an account, and it never applies on your behalf: you open the employer’s own page and apply there.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/clover-pair.png" caption="Clover on web and mobile: one line in, matching jobs out" />

The jobs list shows every open role with filters for the country you work from, workplace, profession, level, English and freshness. Each card says exactly which countries the company hires in.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/jobs-pair.png" caption="Jobs list on web and mobile with the filters" />

A job page puts the facts first: where it hires, workplace, pay, level, stack — each with a note on how it was determined — plus how the pay compares with similar roles and a plain Apply button to the original posting.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/job-detail-pair.png" caption="Job page on web and mobile: the facts, the pay comparison and similar jobs" />

Companies get a sortable table — open and remote roles, where each hires, top stack, when they last posted — and a page of their own.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/companies-pair.png" caption="Companies table on web and mobile" />

The product has a business model now: Free covers the basics, Seeker Pro adds the full AI model, every resume match, instant alerts by email and Telegram, and an MCP server for Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/pricing-pair.png" caption="Pricing on web and mobile: Free and Seeker Pro" />

Around it there is a free Discord community: job leads by country, referrals, salary talk and CV reviews, with a direct line to me as the founder.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/HireClover/community-pair.png" caption="Community page on web and mobile" />

## Result

At the time of writing HireClover tracks around 88,000 open jobs from more than 3,000 companies and checks 160 countries for every role. The site works in six languages, and the whole stack — collectors in Python, search on Meilisearch, PostgreSQL and a Next.js frontend — runs on my own server.
