## Task

I ran into the problem myself: finding a job means digging through dozens of sites, newsletters and job boards, each with its own filters and its own noise. I was spending hours checking everything by hand. So I decided to build one place that pulls the openings together and makes them actually searchable.

## Solution

I did the whole product cycle on my own — from design to development. Collection runs on n8n workflows that parse job sources and write everything into Supabase; the frontend is Next.js.

The feed has full-text search and filters by category, seniority and work format, sorting by date, and a switch between list and grid. The header shows how many jobs are live right now.

Each opening gets its own page: description, company, location, seniority, similar roles — and an Apply button that links straight to the original posting. I deliberately did not lock anything behind the platform: HireClover helps you find the job, then gets out of the way.

## Result

The project started as Znaidy — a board for designers only. After the rebrand to HireClover it grew into a general aggregator: engineering, design, marketing and other roles, remote, hybrid and office, from companies worldwide. At the time of writing there are around 200 live openings in the feed.
