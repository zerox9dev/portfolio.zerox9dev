## Task

Anyone who moved abroad knows the drill: rules on residence permits, taxes and healthcare are scattered across government portals in a language you barely read, retold in Facebook groups by people who guess. The answers exist, but half of them are outdated and none of them say where they came from.

I wanted a reference where every statement carries a source and a date, and where you can quickly check what a move actually costs.

## Solution

Закордон is a reference site for Ukrainians abroad, built around twelve topics — temporary protection, residence permit, taxes, work permit, healthcare, education, banking, housing, social benefits, driving licence, documents and legalisation, consular services. It now covers 48 countries, from the EU to the US, Canada and the UAE.

The site got an editorial redesign in blue and yellow: a search on the first screen (“PESEL”, “residence permit”) and the topics right next to it, so you get to an answer in one or two clicks.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/home-pair.png" caption="Home on web and mobile: search, topics and the number of checked facts" />

Every article is built from fact cards. Each one links to the original source, usually a government portal, and carries its own status: when it was last checked, whether it has gone stale, or whether there is no reliable data yet. That last state matters — an honest “no data” is more useful than a confident guess.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/article-pair.png" caption="Article on web and mobile: key facts as questions, each with a source" />

Temporary protection became its own topic, with a timeline of every extension of the EU directive and a filter by country — the question people ask most often.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/protection-pair.png" caption="Temporary protection timeline on web and mobile" />

On top of the guides there are calculators and comparisons that answer what a move actually costs: whether your income covers living costs, how much of a salary is left after taxes and rent in each country, and a ranking that puts wages, costs and mortgages in one table.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/net-salary-pair.png" caption="Net salary by country on web and mobile: taxes, rent and what is left" />

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/ranking-pair.png" caption="Country ranking on web and mobile" />

## Result

The site is live and gets updated as the rules change — the whole point is that content ages visibly instead of quietly. At the time of writing it holds more than 1,650 checked facts across 48 countries, with a Telegram forum for questions. Built as a static Astro site with Pagefind search, so pages stay fast and cheap to serve at any traffic.
