## Task

Anyone who moved abroad knows the drill: rules on residence permits, taxes and healthcare are scattered across government portals in a language you barely read, retold in Facebook groups by people who guess. The answers exist, but half of them are outdated and none of them say where they came from.

I wanted a reference where every statement carries a source and a date, and where you can quickly check what a move actually costs.

## Solution

Закордон is a reference site for Ukrainians in Europe, built around eleven topics — residence permit, taxes, work permit, healthcare, education, banking, housing, social benefits, driving licence, documents and legalisation, consular services.

Every fact card links to the original source, usually a government portal, and carries its own status: when it was last updated, whether it has gone stale, or whether there is no reliable data yet. That last state matters — an honest "no data" is more useful than a confident guess.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/topic.png" caption="Topic page: countries and facts, each with a source" />

On top of the guides there are calculators that answer the questions people actually ask: whether your income clears the threshold for a permit, how much is left after taxes, when your years add up to permanent residence, and which documents you still need to collect.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/tax-calculator.png" caption="Tax calculator — what is left after income tax" />

And a comparison layer for choosing between countries: an overall score, wages, taxes, cost of living and mortgage availability for foreigners.

<ProjectShot src="/projects/Zakordon/wages.png" caption="Wages across countries — minimum, average and what is left after expenses" />

## Result

The site is live and gets updated as the rules change — the whole point is that content ages visibly instead of quietly. Built as a static Astro site on Vercel, so pages stay fast and cheap to serve at any traffic.
